1. Introduction

Welcome to Up News Uttar Pradesh News ("the App", "we", "us", or "our"), published and operated by Zorif. We are committed to safeguarding your personal privacy while delivering fast, transparent, and accurate news aggregation for the state of Uttar Pradesh and national headlines.

This Privacy Policy informs users of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our Android mobile application. By downloading, accessing, or using Up News Uttar Pradesh News, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information Collection & Use

We believe in minimal data footprint. We do not require you to create an account, log in with social networks, or submit personal details to read news.

A. Personal Information

We do not collect any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, residential address, email address, phone number, contacts list, camera, microphone, or precise GPS location.

B. Local Storage & Offline Data

The App uses your device's local database storage (via Android Room SQLite) solely for:

  • Temporary News Cache: Storing article headlines, summaries, and image URLs to provide fast loading and low bandwidth usage.
  • User Bookmarks: Storing articles you manually choose to "Save" (सहेजी गई खबरें) so you can read them offline.

Note: All bookmarked and cached data remains strictly stored on your physical device. It is never uploaded, transferred, or synced to any external server owned by us.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob & Meta Audience Network)

To keep Up News Uttar Pradesh News 100% free for all readers, we display advertisements supplied by Google Mobile Ads (Google AdMob) and Meta Audience Network through AdMob mediation.

We strictly ensure that ads are displayed in a clean, non-disruptive manner:

  • Banner Advertisements: Displayed at the bottom of the main news feed and the in-app article reader, positioned safely above device navigation bars.
  • Interstitial (Full-Screen) Advertisements: Displayed periodically only after every 4th article clicked. Interstitial ads can always be immediately closed via a visible close button. If an ad takes more than 2 seconds to load, it is skipped automatically so you can read your news without delays.

4. RSS Feed Aggregation & Web View Reader

Up News Uttar Pradesh News is an RSS feed reader and news aggregator. The App fetches publicly available RSS syndication feeds published by leading Hindi news publishers:

  • दैनिक भास्कर (Dainik Bhaskar): bhaskar.com
  • लाइव हिन्दुस्तान (Live Hindustan): livehindustan.com (Lucknow & Varanasi regional editions)
  • अमर उजाला (Amar Ujala): amarujala.com
  • News18 हिंदी (News18 Hindi): hindi.news18.com

When you tap on any news article card, the full original article is opened within an in-app Android WebView (with a clean reader mode for selected sources), loading directly from the publisher's official server.

Please be aware that when browsing external publisher web pages inside the WebView, the publisher's own web servers may use web cookies, analytic tags, or scripts subject to their respective terms and privacy policies. We do not control or access the web data exchanged between your browser and the news publishers.

5. Push Notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging) & In-App Updates

Up News Uttar Pradesh News provides real-time news alerts and seamless application maintenance:

A. Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)

The App utilizes Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM), an infrastructure service provided by Google LLC, to deliver optional daily morning alerts, and important state updates concerning Uttar Pradesh.

  • Pseudonymous Token: To route notifications to your device, a randomized, non-personally identifiable Firebase installation token is generated. This token does not contain personal information (such as your name, email, or phone number) and is used exclusively for transmitting news alerts to the general broadcast topic (daily_readers).
  • Explicit Permission (Android 13+): On Android 13 (API 33) and later versions, the App strictly requests explicit runtime permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) before presenting any notification alerts.
  • User Control & Opt-Out: You may disable news notifications at any time directly through your Android device settings (Settings → Apps → Up News Uttar Pradesh News → Notifications → Turn Off). Disabling notifications stops all broadcast alerts immediately.

B. Google Play Flexible In-App Updates

The App integrates the official Google Play In-App Update library to keep your application up-to-date with the latest security enhancements, performance improvements, and news feed optimizations.

  • Flexible updates download silently in the background while you continue reading news uninterrupted.
  • All update files are securely verified and distributed directly by Google Play infrastructure. No personal or device data is collected or transmitted to external servers by this mechanism.

6. Android Permissions Used

Up News Uttar Pradesh News requires only standard, non-sensitive Android permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Essential for downloading the latest RSS news feeds, fetching news article thumbnails, and loading news articles in the reader.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Allows the App to detect whether your device is connected to Wi-Fi, cellular, or offline, enabling offline bookmark fallback notices.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13 (API 33) and above to display timely updates. This permission is completely optional and can be managed in system settings.

The App does not request permissions for Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Location, SMS, Phone State, or External Storage.

7. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

Because we do not store any personal user data on external servers, you have full and immediate control over your data:

  • Individual Article Deletion: You can un-bookmark any saved news item at any time by tapping the bookmark icon on the article card.
  • Complete Data Erasure: You can clear all cached news and saved articles at any time by going to Settings → Apps → Up News Uttar Pradesh News → Storage → Clear Data, or by uninstalling the application from your device.
