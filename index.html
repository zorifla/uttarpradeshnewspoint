2. Information Collection & Use

We believe in a minimal data footprint. Unlike many news apps, Uttarakhand News does not require any user registration, account sign-up, or login.

A. Zero Personal Information (PII)

We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII), including:

  • Your name, age, gender, or date of birth
  • Your email address or phone number
  • Your contacts or address book
  • Your precise geographic GPS location
  • Your photos, camera, microphone, or external files
  • Financial details or payment information (the app is 100% free)

B. Local Storage & Offline Data

Uttarakhand News utilizes an offline-first local architecture powered by Android's SQLite Room Database and SharedPreferences. The following data is saved strictly on your local device:

  • Temporary News Cache: Caches headlines, short summaries, publication timestamps, and thumbnail URLs so that you can browse news quickly even in weak network areas.
  • User Bookmarks (पसंदीदा): Articles that you manually choose to bookmark or save for later reading.
  • App Preferences: Selected news sources and local UI preferences.

Important: All cached articles and bookmarks are stored exclusively in your device's private app sandbox. They are never uploaded, synchronized, or transmitted to any server owned by us.

5. Android Device Permissions Used

Uttarakhand News requests only minimal, standard, non-sensitive Android permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Essential for downloading official RSS news feeds, fetching article images, and rendering advertisements.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Detects whether your device is connected to Wi-Fi, cellular, or offline, enabling seamless offline bookmark fallback.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: Standard Google Play permission required on Android 13+ to access the resettable Advertising ID for ad frequency capping.

The App does NOT request sensitive permissions for Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Precise GPS Location, SMS, Phone State, or External Storage.
